# Result: fire-spread hindcast (tried, did not pass)

Branch `experiment/spread-sim`, run once on 2026-10-04 with the parameters in PASS_LINE.md (written before the run).
Code: `01_inputs.py` (DEM, Sentinel-2 NDVI, wind), `model.py`, `hindcast.py`. Picture: `hindcast.png` (left simulated, right FIRMS).

| Pass line | Result |
|---|---|
| Simulated fire reaches Summerland's west edge within 3 h of FIRMS | Fail: about 10 h late (Aug 8 06:09 vs Aug 7 20:06) |
| IoU against the FIRMS footprint of 0.4 or better at the end | Fail: 0.12 (recall 0.98, precision 0.12) |

- The simulation overshoots: about 1,800 km2 burned by Aug 10 18:00 against 213 km2 in FIRMS. It has no suppression, burnout or weather change.
- It is too slow in the first hours: FIRMS reaches the west edge of Summerland about 3.3 h after ignition.
- The one calibration knob chose the lowest value offered (0.25), so even that fit is not bracketed. No further tuning was done, to avoid overfitting a single fire.
- Likely causes (not tested): a wind-driven run with spotting that this model cannot make, coarse single-point wind using sustained speed (gusts reached 11 m/s), NDVI as a weak fuel proxy, no containment.
- Not checked: spread across seeds, sensitivity to the ignition radius.
- Deviation: a continuous-time version of the cellular automaton, because 100 m cells with 15-minute steps cap spread at 400 m/h.

Decision: not wired into the web page or the Layer 1 analysis. `sim_detections.csv` (17 MB, output of the failed run, not committed) is regenerated by hindcast.py and must not be used.
