### CBC Article Aug 8 9:15pm
Evacuation orders and alerts have been expanded as a fast-moving wildfire races toward Summerland, B.C., forcing more than 20,000 people to evacuate.

The entire District of Summerland was forced to evacuate in the early hours of Saturday morning just after midnight due to the Bald Range wildfire.

About an hour later, dozens of properties were ordered to evacuate in Peachland and Electoral Area West on Brenda Mine Road and in the Renfrew and Upper Princeton areas.

Dozens more properties in the Peachland watershed and Brenda Mines area, Lower Princeton, and the downtown core were placed on evacuation alert just after 3 a.m. PT.

Highway 97 is closed in both directions for a stretch between Peachland and the Pyramid Picnic Area at Kickininee Provincial Park, just south of Summerland.
The district declared a state of emergency on Saturday, stating the wildfire poses a risk to life and safety.

The fire started about 15 kilometres northwest of Summerland, which is on the southwest end of Okanagan Lake, around 250 kilometres east of Vancouver.

The Bald Range fire grew to 50 square kilometres in three hours after it was first detected at 5:30 p.m. PT on Friday, said Erick Thompson with the Regional District of Okanagan-Similkameen's emergency operations centre.

It had burned over 95 square kilometres as of 8 a.m. PT Saturday.
