Origins are 74 sample points in Summerland. “Cut off” means no drivable route to an exit once nearby roads are removed. Drive times are estimated minimum times (free-flow, no congestion) using OSM speed limits where tagged (about 15% of roads) and default speeds otherwise. Satellites only see fire when they pass over, and no detections were found near Peachland.
